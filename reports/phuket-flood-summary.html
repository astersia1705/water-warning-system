How this was made

Collection

X API v2 recent search, Thai only, no reposts. Query: (ภูเก็ต OR #ภูเก็ต OR ป่าตอง OR กะทู้ OR ฉลอง OR ราไวย์ OR กะรน OR ถลาง) (น้ำท่วม OR น้ำป่า OR ท่วม OR ระดับน้ำ OR ดินสไลด์ OR ดินถล่ม) -is:retweet lang:th

Categorizing

Claude Haiku 5.5 read each post and returned relevance, category, urgency, place and needs as structured data. 63% of collected posts were judged relevant; the rest were off-topic or figurative uses of น้ำท่วม.

Places

Place names in the text (ป่าตอง, ซอยนาใน, คลองบางใหญ่, โคกโตนด and 24 others) are matched to hand-entered centre points. Good for an overview, not for street-level dispatch.

Cost

About $2.20 of X API reads for the 433 posts, plus under $0.10 of Claude usage. Collection keeps running every few minutes for new posts.

Limits

Every report is an unverified public post. Official accounts and news pages that post on X are included; Facebook and LINE, where much of Thai flood talk happens, are not.
